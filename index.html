Methodology & Data

GEE regression (Gamma family, log link) fitted on 800 observations from 495 unique prospects ranked in the FanGraphs Top 100 (2012–2019). Career earnings inflation-adjusted using MLB average salaries normalized to 2025 dollars. Model predictors: Rank (continuous), Position, Age, Highest Minor League Level, and Age × Level interaction. Probabilities derived from Gamma survival function. R² (Corr²) = 0.1936. This tool provides estimates for informational purposes; all investment decisions should incorporate additional due diligence.
