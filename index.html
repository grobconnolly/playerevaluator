    Methodology & Data

    v8 (default): Tweedie GLM (log link) refit on the canonical dataset — 14 fully-observed Top-100 cohorts (2001–2014), 1,357 observations from 797 unique prospects (cluster-robust), fixed 12-year earnings horizon in 2026 dollars, busts included; career-equivalent scaling ×1.444; two-part uncertainty (rank-based bust probability + Gamma among earners, shape 0.886); valuation is level-free (level sets payout duration only). Out-of-sample Spearman 0.364 (train 2001–2011, test 2013–2014). v7 (legacy): GEE regression (Gamma family, log link) fitted on 800 observations from 495 unique prospects ranked in the FanGraphs Top 100 (2012–2019). Career earnings inflation-adjusted using MLB average salaries normalized to 2025 dollars. Model predictors: Rank (continuous), Position, Age, Highest Minor League Level, and Age × Level interaction. Probabilities and percentile ranges derived from the fitted Gamma distribution. R² (Corr²) = 0.1936 — predictions carry substantial uncertainty; the P25–P75 range and P(≥1× Payback) column reflect this. Target offers use upfront-cash NPV pricing: predicted earnings are in real 2025 dollars, discounted at a real rate (base 12%, aggressive 10%, conservative 15%) over the level's earnings-weighted duration (7.2y MLB-debuted to 12.7y CPX/DSL), with a residual margin (1.67–2.22×, +0.28 at CPX/DSL) covering model error, deal costs, and collection risk — every athlete is screened, so no separate information discount applies. Each risk is priced exactly once; bust risk is already in the Gamma mean. Prospect lookup data: FanGraphs "The Board" 2026 report (retrieved July 2026). This tool provides estimates for informational purposes; all investment decisions should incorporate additional due diligence.
